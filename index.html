Featured project: Client and Project Management CRM

A product I built for freelancers and small agencies who juggle clients, deadlines and invoices across too many tools.

The problem
Client details, project status, meeting notes and invoices lived in different places, so nothing gave a clear picture of the work in flight.
What I built
One app covering clients, projects, meetings and invoices, with a Kanban board for project stages, role-based access and a dashboard of key metrics.
Key decisions
Separate roles so team members see only what they need. A board-first layout, because status is the question people ask most. Invoices tied to projects instead of entered separately.
